Beans

An Image of an uncooked Beans. Not my favourite food but it's healthy.

I want to prepare what we call a Nigerian Beans.

Ingredients

  • Palm Oil
  • Onions
  • Fresh Peppers
  • Beans
  • Salts
  • Spices
  • Plantain
  • Cray Fish

Steps to prepare this

  1. Step 1: Perboil the Bean, wash and set aside
  2. Step 2: Slice the fresh pepper, plantain, blend the crayfish and set them aside
  3. Step 3: Wash, cook and the beans until it's almost ready.
  4. Step 4: Pour goundnut oil and all of the blended ingredients into the beans and cook them together until it's ready.
